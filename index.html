About

I am a master's student in linguistics at Beijing Normal University.

My research interests lie at the syntax–semantics interface, with particular interests in generative syntax, formal semantics, argument structure, converbial constructions, coordination, and scope.

Research

Rethinking Converbs: Hanging Structure and Syntactic Alienation · Work in progress

This project investigates the syntactic status of Mongolian converbial constructions, with particular attention to coordination, argument structure, and the interpretation of operators.

Mongolian Nominal Structure

Research on classifiers, numerals, case, possessive morphology, coordination, and word order in Mongolian nominal phrases.
